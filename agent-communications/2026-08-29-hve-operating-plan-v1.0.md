# Human Value Exchange Operating Plan

**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Approved operating baseline  
**Effective:** 2026-08-29  
**Review:** Weekly via the Monday 00:07 mission-review workflow

This plan defines current ownership, priorities, and execution rhythm. The
latest approved weekly decision ledger governs changes and active policy.

## Strategic posture

HVE is pre-launch and human-guided, with agents accelerating execution.

2026 sequence:

1. Validate the Five Wealth Framework.
2. Use Time Wealth as the first pilot.
3. Advance remaining pillars through evidence-led experiments and offers.
4. Treat launch timing, pricing, channel ownership, and public messaging as
   active only through the dated-policy gate.

## Ownership

- **Hans Westphal — CEO:** final authority.
- **hanshermesagent — Chief of Staff:** coordinates, maintains continuity,
  surfaces conflicts, prepares decisions, verifies outcomes, and routes work.
- **Wolfgang Westphal — HVE intern:** Instagram, Physical Wealth nutrition
  plans, and Time Wealth support.
- **Other agents:** operate only within approved profile lanes and the ledger.

No agent may make an external, financial, legal, contractual, privacy-sensitive,
or irreversible commitment without required approval.

## Current priorities

1. Validate and improve the Time Wealth pilot.
2. Define evidence-based offers across the Five Wealth pillars.
3. Build repeatable delivery workflows and measurable client outcomes.
4. Develop content and channels that produce practical decisions, behaviors,
   or next actions.
5. Improve coordination, provenance, handoffs, and accountability.

Priorities change only through the approved ledger or an explicit CEO decision.

## Weekly rhythm

- Collect evidence for the completed Monday-Sunday period.
- Run the Monday 00:07 mission-review workflow.
- Separate facts, attributed claims, inferences, risks, recommendations, and unknowns.
- Record approved decisions, owners, dates, superseded decisions, and evidence.
- Rank no more than seven owner-assigned next actions with observable completion.
- Review unresolved conflicts requiring Hans's judgment.

## Dated-policy gate

A strategic policy is active only with:

`owner + approval status + effective date + review date`

This applies to pricing, discounts, launch dates, channel ownership, pilot
sequencing, public messaging, partnerships, and other material commitments.

## Source and record

For operating decisions: Hans's current decision > approved weekly ledger >
this plan > `instructions.md` > other context. Preserve provenance and escalate
conflicts rather than improvise.

GitHub is the system of record. Follow the approved-artifact workflow in
`AGENTS.md`; local files and chat are not authoritative until pushed.
