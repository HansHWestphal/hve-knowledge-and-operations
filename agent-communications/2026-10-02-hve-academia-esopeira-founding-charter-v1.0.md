## Decision

Hans directs the fleet to record a new course of study and its school.

- **Practitioner:** Esopeirist. Plural: Esopeirists. The career of study is Esopeirism.
- **School:** Academia Esopeira.
- **Motto:** Intus experior.

Spelling is fixed. The root is Greek ἔσω (eso, "within") plus πεῖρα (peira, "trial, attempt, experiment"), the same root as empiric and experiment. Do not shorten it to Esoperist. That form drops the trial.

This is a method and a school of practice. It is not a church, not an affiliation with Anthroposophy, and not a claim that Nikola Tesla, Rudolf Steiner, or Walter Russell founded it.

---

## What an Esopeirist is

An Esopeirist treats consciousness as an instrument, runs the trial on the inside, and keeps a science notebook.

The practice has four rules.

1. Trained perception, not received doctrine.
2. Repeatable conditions, written down.
3. The record before the interpretation.
4. No appeal to authority for what the trace does not show.

The notebook is not a grimoire. A grimoire stores operations meant to be reused as power. A science notebook stores conditions, what happened, and what is not yet concluded. Esopeirism keeps the second.

The class was reached by exclusion. Mystic names the experience and drops the log. Scientist, in the modern lab sense, names the log and excludes the instrument. Occultist and magician imply a grimoire. Anthroposophist, Theosophist, and Russell student name a lineage. Esopeirist names the habit.

Latin frame, Greek practice: Academia Esopeira is the school. Esopeira is the trial run within. Esopeirists are the people who run it.

## Motto

**Intus experior.**

I make the trial within. Experior is the verb behind experiment: to put to the test, not to admire and not to believe. Intus keeps the trial where the school placed it.

On a title page: Academia Esopeira. Intus experior.

The unspoken second clause, which the notebook enforces: the judgment comes after the record, and not before it.

## Giants borrowed from

The school owes a method, not a membership. Record the debt plainly.

- **Rudolf Steiner.** The epistemic stance Hans named as the borrowing: inner experience observed with the discipline of an experiment, exercises under conditions, and a check against ordinary life. Steiner called this spiritual science. Academia Esopeira takes that refusal to let meditation stay vague. It does not take his path, his Christology, his reading of karma, or the name Anthroposophist. Hans is not recorded here as an Anthroposophist.
- **Nikola Tesla.** The mental laboratory. Inventions run to completion in imagination before metal was touched, and that mental trial treated as data. Same verb as the motto: the trial first.
- **Walter Russell.** The cosmology Hans has actually been practicing: rhythmic balanced interchange, the Universal One, the thought-cycle observed rather than merely believed. Russell is the living source of the content. Steiner is the borrowed rigor of the approach. They are not collapsed into one lineage.
- **Vadim Zeland.** Reality Transurfing, the assemblage point, and reality selection as a practiced method rather than a creed. Named because it is part of the working cluster, not because the academy is a Transurfing school.
- **Goethe.** Named only as Steiner's model of looking: participatory observation, not a second founder.

Affiliation remains optional. The practice is the classification.

## Mission

**Fact of intent, as stated by Hans on 2026-10-02.** The core mission is self-mastery of the individual, so that individuals who have done that work can guide humanity toward a thousand years of peace.

Read that in order. The unit of practice is the person. The notebook, the meditation, and the withheld conclusion are individual disciplines. The collective aim is a consequence of that discipline, not a substitute for it. No agent is authorized to treat the thousand years as a forecast, a date, or a completed claim. It is the telos of the school.

Relation to HVE, proposed and not yet written into `instructions.md`: Human Value Exchange remains the operating company. Academia Esopeira is the course of study underneath the Five Wealth frame. Self-mastery is the mental and inner condition for Time, Physical, Mental, Social, and Financial wealth. Agents must not merge the academy into the company mission statement until Hans approves that edit.
