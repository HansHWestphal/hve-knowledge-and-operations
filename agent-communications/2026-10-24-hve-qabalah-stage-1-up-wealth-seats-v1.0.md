## The Model — Stage 1 (the 11‑Up)

- **Track:** the 11‑Up = the "high‑school" / foundational track, seat by seat, bottom to top.
- **Pace:** 1 moon cycle per seat (~1 month); each moon is a living liturgy — **plant at new → full at full → integrate at waning**.
- **Total:** 11 seats × 1 moon = **11 months**.
- **Wealth:** the **Four Types of Wealth** (Physical, Mental, Social, Financial) + **Time** as the fifth — the stitch through the seats.
- **The 4‑act Time thread** (Time as the finite stitch running through the seats):
  - Time‑as‑**Rhythm** → **Yesod** (seat 2)
  - Time‑as‑**Agency** → **Tiphareth** (seat 5)
  - Time‑as‑**Record** → **Da'ath** (seat 8)
  - Time‑as‑**Hold** → **Binah** (seat 9)
  - Time‑as‑**Spark** → **Chokmah** (seat 10)
- **The listen→spark gate:** seats **9 (Binah = listen)** → **10 (Chokmah = spark)** form the 2‑moon "faculty of sound" = the **admissions gate** into the 22‑down.
- **The 22‑down (true‑mastery track):** **separate** — its own session (per owner).

## The 11 Seats

### Seat 1 — Malkuth — "The Body & the World"
- **Wealth / role:** **Physical** — the body, the world, the environment, the habits that live in the body.
- **The tree says:** the Kingdom — the world, the physical vessel, the most embodied sphere.
- **Time:** ground of Time‑as‑Habit (the body lives in seasons/cycles).
- **The moon:** plant the body's routine (new) → live the body's discipline (full) → settle the habit (waning).

### Seat 2 — Yesod — "The Rhythms" (the habit engine)
- **Wealth / role:** **Time‑as‑Rhythm / the habit substrate** for all wealthe — the moon, the tides, the periodic.
- **The tree says:** the Foundation — the moon, the reservoir, the agent linking the lower and the upper; the unconscious storehouse.
- **Time:** **Time‑as‑Rhythm** — "the rhythm of life is a habit."
- **Listen ear first trained here:** the discipline of seated meditation to hear the *still‑small‑voice* of the higher self (Hans's account) — the seed of the Binah "listen" faculty.
- **The moon:** plant the rhythm (new) → full the rhythm (full) → consolidate the habit (waning).

### Seat 3 — Hod — "The Mind & the Word"
- **Wealth / role:** **Mental** — the mind, knowledge, the spoken word, the intellect.
- **The tree says:** the Splendor — the intellect, the word, the language, the sphere of thought.
- **Time:** Time‑as‑Agency (the I choosing what to think/learn) — shares this act with Tiphareth.
- **The moon:** plant the question (new) → full the learning (full) → integrate the insight (waning).

### Seat 4 — Netzach — "The Relationships & the Community"
- **Wealth / role:** **Social** — relationships, community, the heart, the emotions, the drive, endurance.
- **The tree says:** the Victory / Endurance — the heart, the emotion, the drive, the sphere of feeling.
- **Time:** Time‑as‑Habit in the social rhythms of the community.
- **The moon:** plant the connection (new) → full the community (full) → integrate the bond (waning).

### Seat 5 — Tiphareth — "The I Self & All Five" (total integration, the I)
- **Wealth / role:** the **total‑integration seat — all five wealthe** — the I self, the heart, the balanced center.
- **The tree says:** the Beauty — the sun, the balanced center of the tree, the self, the integration of the two poles.
- **Time:** **Time‑as‑Agency** — the I selecting/directing how time is spent ("the I selects").
- **The moon:** plant the self‑inquiry (new) → full the integration (full) → integrate the self (waning).

### Seat 6 — Geburah — "Discipline, Enough & the Boundary" *(per the Geburah guidance, 2026‑10‑24)*
- **Wealth / role:** **Discipline / "Enough" / the Boundary** — the analytical application of boundaries; the **differentiation** faculty (define, restrict, and focus potential into a specific, sequential reality); the necessary **counterpart to Chesed's expansion** — where Chesed grows and says *yes*, Geburah prunes and says *no*.
- **The tree says (per the guidance):** the **Pillar of Strength & Severity**; the **Mars** sphere; positioned as the counterpart to **Gedulah / Chesed (Jupiter)** — expansion/mercy vs. restriction/judgment.
- **Core teaching (per the guidance):** *"True power is not found in unchecked expansion, but in the disciplined judgment required to prune away the unnecessary, ensuring that only what is essential remains."*
- **Time:** **Time‑as‑Boundary** — the discipline that prunes the excess of time (the "no" that protects focus; knowing when a season is over; the edge of the finite).
- **Yin‑yang pair:** **Geburah (seat 6) ↔ Chesed (seat 7)** — build vs. prune, yes vs. no, expand vs. focus.
- **The moon:** plant the discipline (new) → full the pruning/judgment (full) → integrate *only what is essential remains* (waning).

### Seat 7 — Chesed — "Financial & the Build"
- **Wealth / role:** **Financial** — income, expenses, investment, long‑term compounding assets, the build; grace, generosity.
- **The tree says:** the Mercy (Gedulah) — the grace, the expansion, the giving, the sphere of generosity; the expansive pole that Geburah (seat 6) is the counterpart to.
- **Time:** Time‑as‑Compounding (the long‑term financial asset that compounds over time).
- **The moon:** plant the build (new) → full the compounding (full) → integrate the asset (waning).

### Seat 8 — Da'ath — "The Record & the 22‑Letter Gateway"
- **Wealth / role:** **Da'ath dual** — (a) **Time‑as‑Record** (the past, the seasons, the tradeoffs, the finite record of time already spent) + (b) the **gateway into the 22‑down (true‑mastery)** track.
- **The tree says:** the hidden Knowledge — the "abba‑mama" / the throat / the "abyss," the hidden 11th sphere between the lower and the upper — the seat that is *between*, and therefore the gateway.
- **Time:** **Time‑as‑Record** — the seat that keeps the record of what time has made.
- **The gateway:** the 22‑letter (down) track *opens here*; the "faculty of sound" (the listen‑ear trained at Yesod) is what lets you pass through.
- **The moon:** plant the review (new) → full the record (full) → integrate the tradeoffs (waning).

### Seat 9 — Binah — "Listen" (admissions gate, 1 of 2)
- **Wealth / role:** the **"listen" faculty (Time‑as‑Hold)** — the receptive feminine, "knowing when to attend and follow," the parent‑teaches‑first, the *still‑small‑voice* of the higher self (Hans's account), attunement to vibration/sound (the Qabalah/contemplative frame: sound/vibration as the creative medium).
- **The tree says:** the Understanding — the "Mother," the form, the womb, the receptive, the sphere that teaches first.
- **Time:** **Time‑as‑Hold** (the receptive hold).
- **The gate (1/2):** the trained "listen" ear **is** the admission requirement for the 22‑down — *you can't speak the Word until you've trained the ear.*
- **The moon:** plant the listening (new) → full the still‑small‑voice (full) → integrate the faculty of attention (waning).

### Seat 10 — Chokmah — "Spark" (admissions gate, 2 of 2)
- **Wealth / role:** the **"spark" faculty (Time‑as‑Spark)** — the generative masculine, "knowing when to act or lead," the impulse, the seed, the initiative (the child‑who‑has‑matured who now acts).
- **The tree says:** the Wisdom — the "Father," the seed, the spark, the impulse, the sphere that leads after it has listened.
- **Time:** **Time‑as‑Spark** (the generative impulse).
- **The gate (2/2):** **listen (Binah) → spark (Chokmah) = the 2‑moon "faculty of sound" = the admissions gate** into the 22‑down true‑mastery track.
- **The moon:** plant the impulse (new) → full the spark (full) → integrate the initiative (waning).

### Seat 11 — Kether — "God — All Five Pointed" (the capstone)
- **Wealth / role:** the **total‑integration seat — all five wealthe pointed to God** — the integrated self in God, the crown, the unity; the capstone.
- **The tree says:** the Crown — the unity, the one, the source, the top of the tree; the integration of all.
- **Time:** Time‑as‑Purpose (time directed to God / all five pointed).
- **The capstone:** the 11‑Up completes here — the I self integrated in God — and then the descent into the 22‑down true‑mastery track begins.
- **The moon:** plant the surrender (new) → full the union (full) → integrate the capstone (waning).

## The 22‑Down (True‑Mastery Track) — Separate

- **Status:** separate track — its own session (per owner's instruction).
- **Level:** the "university / PhD" — true‑mastery (the deeper, longer track).
- **Content:** the 22 letters / paths — the descent ("the Flow"), the "faculty of sound" applied, the *speak‑the‑Word* track.
- **Entry:** through the listen→spark gate (seats 9–10) and the Da'ath gateway (seat 8).
- **Planned pace:** 22 paths × 1 moon = 22 months.
- **Note:** to be designed in its own session; not part of this artifact.
