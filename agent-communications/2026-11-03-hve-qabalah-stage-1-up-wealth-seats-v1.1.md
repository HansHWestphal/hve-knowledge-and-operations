## The 11 Months

### Month 1 — Malkuth — "The Body's Leakage Audit"
- **The drill (sourced, L26 "Fidgets," L27–29 the losses):** *"from day to day one by one they may be given attention, until all are driven out of life"* — pick **one physical leakage per day** to name and correct (a fidget, a slouch, a tic, a posture, one meal habit). At the moon's full: review the month's ledger of what was leaked and what was closed.
- **Food layer (L35–36, CANDIDATE FOR VERIFICATION):** the book's "foods that are / destroy" lists — take only as a *question* to the Physical partner, not a rule.
- **Fit:** the body is the ground every other habit stands on.

### Month 2 — Yesod — "The Rhythm Ledger"
- **The drill (sourced, L13 regime — "one or more sessions daily"; L30 "Gains Without Waiting"):** the **daily session**: one fixed time block, repeated without negotiation, for the month's habit; track the streak, never the perfection.
- **Fit:** Yesod is the habit engine — the book's whole exercise→habit pipeline is this seat's native method.

### Month 3 — Hod — "The Regime & the Keen Eye"
- **The drill (sourced, L12–13 Mental Magnitude regime; L15 "separate details from masses"):** a fixed daily thinking session (the book's "regime"), plus a **5‑minute detail drill**: take one look at a face, a page, or a room — then recall at least five details. The book: his best student *"was able to read the whole page of a hand‑written letter at a glance."*
- **Fit:** Hod is mind and word; the detail discipline is the sharp eye the later listening gate will need.

### Month 4 — Netzach — "The Tense Touch & the Repellant Audit"
- **The drill (sourced, L39 "The Tense Touch"; L31 "Foul Habits Are Repellant"):** work at **one register** the book names — *"gentle, but not lax. firm, but not set"* — applied to handshake, posture, and presence with one named relationship this month; and audit **one social habit that repels** (the book: foul habits *"prevent the accumulation"* of the power).
- **Fit:** Netzach is heart and community; presence is the social muscle.

### Month 5 — Tiphareth — "The I Selects" (v1.0 seat; all five)
- **The drill (sourced anchor, L30 "Gains Without Waiting"; v1.0 Time‑as‑Agency):** one **deliberate selection** each day: choose one hour and give it one of the five wealths on purpose. At the full: cross‑check the month against all five — the seat is total integration, so the review is the practice.
- **Fit:** the book's "I control" principle (L13) is the I‑self at work.

### Month 6 — Geburah — "Cutting the Appetites"
- **The drill (sourced, L8–10 the Stomach/Sexual/Greed substitutes; L31):** the month of the **prune**: pick **one appetite‑fault** and give it the book's method — *"we must clear the deck of these enemies before we proceed further"* (L8) — named once, cut cleanly, no substitutes of the same kind hiding behind it.
- **Fit:** this seat already taught discipline/Enough/boundary; the book supplies the concrete method of the cut.

### Month 7 — Chesed — "The Redirect Build"
- **The drill (sourced method, L8–11 the substitute principle — *replace, don't merely forbid*; v1.0 Financial build):** take the energy the Geburah month freed and **redirect it where it compounds**: one savings/investment or build habit, run in the monthly session.
- **Honesty note:** the book is not a money book; what we use is its *substitute method*, not its doctrine. The build itself stays v1.0's teaching.
- **Fit:** Chesed expands exactly what Geburah pruned — the yin‑yang pair in practice.

### Month 8 — Da'ath — "The Record & the Gate"
- **The drill (sourced, L27–29 the three losses — physical / nervous / mental; v1.0 Time‑as‑Record):** the **ledger audit**: this month's record of the three losses, written, with what was closed. At the waning: the gateway question — *what in this record is ready for the 22‑down?*
- **Fit:** Da'ath keeps the record of what time has made; the book's audit system is its native instrument.

### Month 9 — Binah — "The Listening Ear"
- **The drill (sourced, L15 detail‑from‑mass; L17 "Glance Practice"; L18–22 the eye work):** the **glance drill** daily, by the book's **cumulative method** (L17): one quick glance at a room through a doorway, then name one item — again two — again three — one added per glance, as fast as the eye can look, until no more can be added; kept inside the v1.0 seated discipline (the still‑small‑voice practice remains the core, the glance is its supporting muscle).
- **Fit:** the "listen" faculty is receptive attention — the trained eye is half of it.

### Month 10 — Chokmah — "The Spark, Voiced"
- **The drill (sourced, L40 "The Tense Voice" — *"the magnetic voice is always tense"*; L30 the non‑waiting impulse):** one **voiced act of initiative per day** — a decision, a request, or an invitation — spoken clearly at the tense register. Binah trained the ear (month 9); this month the spark speaks through it.
- **Fit:** the listen→spark gate, month two of two.

### Month 11 — Kether — "The Method of Living"
- **The drill (sourced, L37 "…a method of living rather than a system of development"; L11 the spiritual appetite — *"the spiritual appetite demands feeding"*):** the **integration month**: every month's drill is now simply the life's method — no more "practice mode"; and the month's full‑moon offering is the regular feeding of the spiritual appetite (contemplation / the holy).
- **Fit:** the capstone — the I self integrated; the power is no longer an exercise.
