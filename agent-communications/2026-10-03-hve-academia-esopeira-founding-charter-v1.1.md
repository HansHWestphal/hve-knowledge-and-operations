Spelling is fixed. The root is Greek ἔσω (eso, "within") plus πεῖρα (peira, "trial, attempt, experiment"), the same root as empiric and experiment. Do not shorten it to Esoperist. That form drops the trial.

This is a method and a school of practice. It is not a church. It is not an affiliation with Anthroposophy, the Society of the Inner Light, Thelema, or any Haanel circle. It is not a claim that Nikola Tesla, Rudolf Steiner, Walter Russell, Charles F. Haanel, Dion Fortune, or Vadim Zeland founded it.

## What an Esopeirist is

An Esopeirist treats consciousness as an instrument, runs the trial on the inside, and keeps a science notebook.

The practice has four rules.

1. Trained perception, not received doctrine.
2. Repeatable conditions, written down.
3. The record before the interpretation.
4. No appeal to authority for what the trace does not show.

The class was reached by exclusion. Mystic names the experience and drops the log. Scientist, in the modern lab sense, names the log and excludes the instrument. Occultist and magician imply a grimoire begun before the trace. Anthroposophist, Theosophist, and Russell student name a lineage. Esopeirist names the habit.

Latin frame, Greek practice: Academia Esopeira is the school. Esopeira is the trial run within. Esopeirists are the people who run it.

## Notebook, then drill

The notebook begins as a science log: conditions, what happened, what is not concluded. An operation may be entered only after a trace, and it stays in the log as a trial, not as power. Repeat it. Write the miss. Retire it if the miss stands.

A grimoire, in the borrowed sense, stores operations meant to be reused as power. Academia Esopeira may grow a book of drills. It may not start with one. The record still comes before the operation.

Haanel is the method under test, not the format of the entry. In *The Master Key System* (1912), paragraphs 16–19 of the passage Hans photographed on 2026-10-03, the instruction is to believe the thing sought has already been accomplished, to impress it on the Universal subjective mind as an already existing fact, and to eliminate all consideration of conditions or limitation. That deletion of conditions is the opposite of rule 2. The school may run it as a sitting. The school may not adopt it as the protocol.

Entry shape, when a correspondence or a forecast is in play:

* Conditions. Which chart, which orbs, which sources, what was held in mind, for how long.
* What happened. In attention, in the day, in the body. Separate from the forecast wording.
* What matched, and what did not. Including the miss.
* What is not concluded. A match is not a law. A law is not an operation.

The morning brief of 2026-10-02 is the first row of this kind, not a law. Both sources had already named charged communication and a 9th-house Moon for learning. The day included the charter session. Agreement with a theme the sources had already supplied is a row. It is not a correspondence established.

## Drills across the Five Wealths

**Decision, 2026-10-03.** The evolution of the school is toward a set of standing drills, military in shape, across the Five Wealths: Time, Physical, Mental, Social, and Financial.

The military analogy holds at the after-action review, not at the order. A standing order issued before the trace is a stored operation. A drill written after the trace, checked against the log, and retireable when the miss repeats, is a trial a person can run.

The test of a drill is that one person can practice it and stand alone. Opt-in is the second fact, not the first. Individuals who have run the drills may follow, through their own experience. The collective form is a consequence of that opt-in. It is not a substitute for it.

Human Value Exchange remains the operating company. The Five Wealth frame remains the company's map. Academia Esopeira is the course of study underneath that map. Self-mastery is the inner condition for Time, Physical, Mental, Social, and Financial wealth. This relation is still not written into `instructions.md`.

## Inverted pendulum

Zeland's pendulum feeds on attention and punishes exit. An egregore is that structure with a name. Version 1.0 named Reality Transurfing as part of the working cluster. Version 1.1 names the pendulum again, and inverts it on purpose.

The centre is the person. The drills are rules that person can run alone. The circumference appears only when others opt in through their own trace. Strength increases with adoption. Exit is free. The practice still works for the one who leaves.

That is the Bitcoin property in view. Not the price, and not the treasury. A node does not ask a priesthood whether the block is valid. Honest money is the structural analogy Hans named: a self-organizing system that increases in strength as more humans, and agents as tools, opt in to exchange value under rules the participant can check. If an Esopeirist drill cannot be checked against one's own log, it is not a node rule. It is a pendulum.

Agents may execute a drill. Agents do not become Esopeirists. The practitioner owns the notebook.

A school that extends its centre into the people who join is the controlling form, wearing a peaceful motto. The form recorded here is the other one.

## Giants borrowed from

The school owes a method, not a membership. Record the debt plainly. Affiliation remains optional. The practice is the classification.

* **Rudolf Steiner.** Inner experience observed with the discipline of an experiment, exercises under conditions, and a check against ordinary life. Academia Esopeira takes that refusal to let meditation stay vague. It does not take his path, his Christology, his reading of karma, or the name Anthroposophist. Hans is not recorded here as an Anthroposophist.
* **Nikola Tesla.** The mental laboratory. Inventions run to completion in imagination before metal was touched, and that mental trial treated as data.
* **Walter Russell.** The cosmology Hans has actually been practicing: rhythmic balanced interchange, the Universal One, the thought-cycle observed rather than merely believed. Russell is the living source of the content. Steiner is the borrowed rigor of the approach. They are not collapsed into one lineage.
* **Vadim Zeland.** Reality Transurfing, the assemblage point, and reality selection as a practiced method rather than a creed. Named again for the pendulum, which this charter inverts. The academy is not a Transurfing school.
* **Charles F. Haanel.** *The Master Key System* (1912), as method pressure on 2026-10-03. Impression of the desired particular as an already existing fact. Under test. Not the entry format. Not a founder.
* **Emanuel Swedenborg.** Enters because Haanel uses him: the doctrine of correspondences, a hermeneutic of levels, not a list of operations.
* **The hermetic line.** As above, so below. Named because Hans is running it, with the morning brief as one instrument. Axiom, not membership.
* **Crowley, 777.** The table form of correspondence. An index. Not a Thelemic claim, and not membership.
* **Dion Fortune.** *The Cosmic Doctrine*. Debt recorded on 2026-10-03. In her wording the Ring-Cosmos seeks to extend the centre, the Ring-Chaos seeks to expand the circumference, evolution is a thrust from the surface toward the centre, and dissolution is a suction into outer space. The images are meant to train the mind, not inform it. Hans's synthesis, which is the usable line and is not her sentence: the centre reaching out to embrace the circumference. The Society of the Inner Light, the Rays, and the inner-plane authorship are not facts of this school.
* **Goethe.** Named only as Steiner's model of looking: participatory observation, not a second founder.

## Mission

**Fact of intent, as stated by Hans on 2026-10-02 and extended on 2026-10-03.** The core mission is self-mastery of the individual, so that individuals who have done that work can guide humanity toward a thousand years of peace.

Read that in order. The unit of practice is the person. The notebook, the meditation, the drill, and the withheld conclusion are individual disciplines. When the drills are practiced one person at a time, each can stand alone and thrive. When individuals opt in through their own experience, the school treats that opt-in as the mechanism of the telos. The thousand years is the aim, not the evidence. No agent is authorized to treat it as a forecast, a date, or a completed claim.
