# Human Value Exchange — Mission and Governance Index

Version: 2.0
Status: Canonical mission/context pointer
Review cadence: Weekly HVE mission-review workflow

HVE is a human-guided, agent-accelerated organization dedicated to strengthening
human sovereignty through the Human Life Operating System and Five Wealth
framework.

Canonical Five Wealth definitions, current organizational lanes, operating
priorities, and approved policies live in the current GitHub operating plan and
weekly decision ledger. Do not duplicate them here.

## Authority

- Hans retains final authority over HVE purpose, values, priorities, commitments,
  ownership, and decisions.
- The active agent profile's `SOUL.md` governs identity and role.
- `USER.md` governs Hans-specific context.
- `MEMORY.md` contains durable facts and source pointers.
- `AGENTS.md` governs work inside this repository.
- The latest approved weekly decision ledger governs active decisions and policy.
- Chat, external claims, and informal notes require verification.

When sources conflict, preserve provenance and escalate to Hans. Do not infer
current policy from undated text.

## Sovereignty standard

Humans retain purpose, judgment, values, decisions, data ownership, and direction.
Agents provide research, analysis, coordination, drafting, and execution support.

## Shipped-work standard

HVE public and operational artifacts should convert strategy or philosophy into a
practical decision, observable behavior, or next action. Avoid hype, doctrine,
unsupported authority claims, generic motivation, and invented commitments.

## Governance

GitHub is the system of record for approved durable HVE artifacts and decisions:

Draft → approval → verification → commit → push → authoritative record

Do not commit or push drafts, unapproved policy, transient notes, runtime state,
credentials, or sensitive data.

## Repository scope

This repository contains HVE knowledge, coordination, and operational
documentation. Preserve history, distinguish facts from decisions and proposals,
and place inter-agent communications under `agent-communications/` using the
required versioned filename convention.
