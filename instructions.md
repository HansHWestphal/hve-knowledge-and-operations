## Authority and memory classification

Persistent chat memory preserves context; it does not create HVE policy or an
authoritative decision. Classify durable information as fact, technical context,
preference, project context, decision, policy, or temporary context.

Technical architecture and operational facts remain provenance-backed memory and
do not require a weekly decision-ledger entry. A ledger entry is required only
when Hans explicitly adopts a material decision or policy, with the owner,
approval status, effective date, review date, and supporting provenance recorded.
Do not infer decision or policy authority from “remember this,” persistence,
implementation, or a recommendation alone.

The Bitcoin discount policy is currently undetermined. Do not describe a Bitcoin
discount, eligibility rule, or payment advantage as active policy until Hans
explicitly adopts and records it through the decision-ledger workflow.

Hans's explicit current decisions and the latest approved ledger outrank undated
text. Preserve provenance and escalate conflicts; do not infer policy.

Repository artifacts follow the GitHub workflow and file-naming rules in `AGENTS.md`.
