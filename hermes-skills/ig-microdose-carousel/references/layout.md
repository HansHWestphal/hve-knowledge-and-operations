# Slide layout

Canvas 1080 x 1350, sRGB.

Colors
- bg `#111111`
- cream `#F3EDE0`
- gold `#C4A574`
- mute `#8A8580`

Safe zones
- Keep type inside x=80..1000, y=160..1180
- Cover and close photos keep the lower 280px free for type
- Grid thumbnail eats the top and sides — no essential words in the outer 48px

Type (DejaVu defaults)
- Hook on cover 56–64px cream
- Subhook 36px gold
- Teaching line slides 2–6 52–58px cream, max ~12 words
- Attribution 28px gold
- Footer 22px mute `KNOW THYSELF`
- Slide 8 keyword 40px gold

Series
- transurfing bed + street / stance covers
- russell bed + quieter covers, workbench, nature proof

Do not change fonts mid-series.
