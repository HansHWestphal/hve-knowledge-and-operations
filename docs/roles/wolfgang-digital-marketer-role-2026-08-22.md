## Weekly Operating System (10 hours / week)

### Monday — Content Distribution Day (90 min)
**Review:** Latest Substack article · recent Hans LinkedIn post · any new program announcement.
**Extract:** 1 key insight · 1 client problem · 1 practical takeaway.
**Build queue:** 3 LinkedIn posts · 2 Instagram posts · 1 Substack promotion. All **repurposed** from existing HVE content — no from-scratch writing.

### Tuesday — LinkedIn Growth Day (90 min)
**Prospect research (45 min):** Find SMB owners / consultants / accountants / insurance advisors / tech & ops leaders in GTA → Ontario → Canada → Microsoft-adjacent. Target: 10 new quality connections.
**Engagement (45 min):** Thoughtful comments on 10 LinkedIn posts. **Rules: add value, never sell, never pitch.** Goal = visibility in target networks.

### Wednesday — Newsletter Growth Day (90 min)
**Substack promote (45 min):** Turn latest article into 1 LinkedIn summary post + 1 Instagram carousel + 1 discussion question. Every one links back to HVE website or Substack.
**Community outreach (45 min):** 5 targeted 1-to-1 messages (e.g. *"Thought this article on Time Wealth might resonate with you."*) — pure relationship, no selling.

### Thursday — Instagram + Brand Day (90 min)
**Publish (45 min):** 1 graphic · 1 quote card · 1 short reel. Themes: Time Wealth · Five Wealth Framework · AI Productivity · HVE Life OS · Sovereignty.
**Engage (45 min):** Reply to comments/DMs/mentions + like/comment on 10 relevant accounts.

### Friday — Funnel Review Day (90 min)
**CRM update (45 min):** Log every person into a simple tracker. Fields: Name · Source · Interest · Follow-up date · Stage. Stages: Curious → Engaged → Discovery → Opportunity → Active.
**Report to Hans (45 min):** One-page weekly summary — see template (Appendix A).

### Remaining ~5 hours — Flex time
Use for: prospecting depth when a promising lead appears, A/B testing copy, deeper research on a specific persona, or backfilling any day that fell short. Log hours against the specific task.

**Total: ~7 hrs fixed block + ~3 hrs flex = 10 hrs/week, minimum documented.**

## Test & Learn — 3 Marketing Experiments (90-Day)

The rest of 2026 is a validation window before the Jan 1, 2027 launch. Wolfgang's sprint doubles as HVE's marketing experiments. He runs (and reports on) three:

**Experiment A — Channel mix:** Where do real conversations start?
Track conversation-initiation rate per channel (LinkedIn vs. Instagram vs. Substack promote). End of sprint → Hans decides where to double down for launch.

**Experiment B — Persona mix:** Which ICPs actually reply?
SMB owner vs. consultant vs. accountant vs. insurance advisor vs. ops leader. Log response rate by persona in the CRM tracker. Kill weak personas, double down on strong ones.

**Experiment C — Hook mix:** What resonates?
Time Wealth framing vs. Five Wealth Framework framing vs. AI Productivity framing vs. HVE Life OS framing. Log engagement + reply rate per hook.

Each Friday report answers a standing question: *proven → scale · learning → iterate · dead → kill.*

## Compensation Structure

### Base Pay
- **$17.20 CAD/hr** (Ontario minimum wage)
- **Minimum 10 hrs/week** → ~**$700–$750 CAD/month**
- Extra hours above 10 logged and paid at same rate, max 15 hr/week without written approval from Hans
- Time logged per session (simple log: task + hours)

### Ramp Bonus (Monthly — replaces old flat Tier 1/Tier 2)

Bonuses paid **each month end** when ramp targets are hit. Paid on top of base pay — never in place of it.

| Month | Discovery Calls Logged | Weekly KPIs Met | Bonus |
|-------|----------------------|-----------------|-------|
| **Month 1** | **1 qualified** | All weekly minimums met for 4 of 5 weeks | **$100 CAD** |
| **Month 2** | **2 qualified** | All weekly minimums met for 4 of 5 weeks | **$250 CAD** |
| **Month 3** | **1 per week** (4 of 4 weeks) | All weekly minimums met for all 4 weeks | **$500 CAD** |
| **Month 4+** (if role continues) | 4–6 qualified / month | KPIs sustain Month-3 baseline | **$750 CAD/month**, review-then-renew quarterly |

**Qualification criteria for a "1 qualified discovery":**
- Prospect is a SMB owner / consultant / accountant / insurance advisor / ops & leadership (fits ICP)
- 15+ minute conversation completed
- Named HVE (or one of its programs) at least once
- Follow-up date set in CRM tracker

**What does NOT count:**
- Passive engagement (likes, follows, comments without a follow-up set)
- Conversations under 15 minutes
- Prospects outside the ICP

### Junior Partner Path (Optional, Month 4+)
If Wolfgang sustains Month-3 KPI baseline for a full quarter + demonstrates autonomy in strategy, he's eligible to enter a Junior Partner discussion tied to HVE revenue attribution. **Not automatic, not promised** — depends on proven business creation.

**Indicative 90-day total (base + bonuses, at ~10 hr/wk):** roughly **$2,064 base + $850 bonuses ≈ $2,914 CAD**, i.e. ~$24/hr blended over the logged hours. Extras above 10 hr/wk grow base proportionally.
